# ACE: Executive Summary & Quick Reference

## ONE-PAGE EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### THE IDEA
**Avatar Content Engine (ACE)** is a **creator OS** that encodes your style into reusable rules (Style Packs), generates variants of every video automatically, scores them on your terms (Eval scorecard), and improves over time via feedback loops.

### THE GAP
- ✅ AI can generate videos (HeyGen, Synthesia)
- ✅ Tools can schedule and analyze (Postiz, Later)
- ❌ **No one ensures consistency + learns from performance**

### THE MOAT
1. **Style as Code** – Style Packs are portable, versioned, human-editable
2. **Evals as Feedback** – Built-in scoring predicts what works
3. **Compounding Learning** – Each upload teaches the system (competitors can't access this data)

### THE WEDGE
Start with **explainer creators** (50K-500K followers, high volume, consistent style = competitive advantage)

### THE MARKET
- TAM: $3.4B (AI video generation market by 2033)
- SAM: $140M-700M (explainers + agencies + brands)
- SOM: $5M-35M ARR (realistic 5-year target)
