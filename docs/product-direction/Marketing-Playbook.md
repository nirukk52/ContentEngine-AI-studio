# ACE: Go-to-Market Strategy & Marketing Playbook

## TARGET AUDIENCE DEFINITION

### Primary (Wedge): Explainer Creators
**Profile:** "Sarah"
- **Age:** 28-35
- **Followers:** 50K-500K (most common: 100K-300K)
- **Platform:** YouTube (primary), TikTok (secondary)
- **Content Type:** Educational/explainer videos (finance, tech, health, careers)
- **Upload Frequency:** 1-4 videos/week
- **Pain Point:** "My 50th video looks worse than my 10th. Style drifts."
- **Current Tools:** 
  - HeyGen (painful manual styling)
  - Postiz (analytics blind)
  - Adobe Premiere (full editing, overkill)
- **Income:** $2K-10K/month (Adsense, sponsorships, Patreon)
- **Budget:** $200-400/month for tools
- **Success Metric:** "Grow from 100K to 500K followers while maintaining brand"

### Secondary (Expansion): Agencies
**Profile:** "Alex" (Agency founder)
- **Team Size:** 5-20 creators
- **Brands Managed:** 2-10 brands
- **Monthly Output:** 40-200 videos
- **Pain Point:** "I need each brand's videos to look consistent, but creators have different styles"
- **Current Tools:** n8n, Postiz, HeyGen (cobbled together)
- **Budget:** $500-2000/month
- **Success Metric:** "Reduce production time from 4 hours to 45 minutes per video"

### Tertiary (Year 2): In-House Brand Teams
**Profile:** "Chris" (Content marketing lead at SaaS)
- **Team Size:** 1-5 internal creators
- **Monthly Output:** 20-50 videos
- **Pain Point:** "Our videos for product demos/sales enablement need consistency"
- **Current Tools:** Synthesia, Descript, Vidyard
- **Budget:** $500-5K/month
- **Success Metric:** "Improve demo booking rate from 12% to 18% with better video quality"

## ORGANIC MARKETING PLAYBOOK

### Content Pillar 1: "Style Studies"
**Goal:** Position ACE as the expert in creator style

**Content Ideas:**
1. **Blog Series:** "Why [Creator]'s Videos Feel Like [Them]"
   - "Why MrBeast's videos feel like MrBeast" (SEO: 500 words)
   - "Why Varun Maya's edits are iconic" (SEO: 500 words)
   - "Why Ali Abdaal videos are consistent" (SEO: 500 words)

2. **YouTube Videos:** "Reverse Engineering Creator Styles"
   - Pick a viral creator
   - Pause on key decisions (hook, pacing, captions)
   - Explain the pattern
   - "This creator probably uses rules like ACE Style Packs"

3. **Twitter/X:** Daily style breakdowns
   - "3 things MrBeast nailed in this video that you should copy"
   - Thread format, 5-10 tweets

### Content Pillar 2: "Creator Tooling"
**Goal:** Position ACE as essential infrastructure

**Content Ideas:**
1. **Free Templates:**
   - Scene Map template (Google Doc)
   - Eval scorecard template (CSV)
   - Style Pack template (JSON example)

2. **Guides:**
   - "Scene Mapping 101: The Creator's Storyboard"
   - "Evals That Actually Work: Predicting Performance"
   - "Building a Style Pack: Step by Step"

3. **Tools:**
   - Free style extractor (web app, basic)
   - Style Pack validator (check JSON syntax)

### Content Pillar 3: "Analytics Deep Dives"
**Goal:** Show ACE evals correlate with performance

**Content Ideas:**
1. **Blog:** "We analyzed 100 explainer videos. Here's what works."
   - Hook strength → CTR correlation
   - Caption density → retention correlation
   - Pacing → watch time correlation

2. **Video:** "Your Eval Scorecard vs. Real YouTube Performance"
   - Show side-by-side comparisons
   - "This video scored 8.5 in our eval and got 3x views"

3. **Report:** Quarterly "Creator Style Benchmark"
   - PDF download (email capture)
   - "Q1 2026: Which style rules actually matter?"
